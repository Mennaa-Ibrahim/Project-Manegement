## 📚 Books & Reading List

_A curated collection of must-read books for anyone looking to master Product and Project Management—from foundational concepts to advanced execution strategies._

### 1. Project Management Fundamentals 📊

* 📕 [A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK Guide)](https://www.academia.edu/63225348/A_guide_to_the_project_management_body_of_knowledge) _by Project Management Institute (PMI)_ — (The global standard and foundational reference for project management principles).
* 📕 [Project Management for Non-Project Managers](https://tech.wayne.edu/project-management/pm-training-non-managers.pdf) — (A practical guide to executing projects successfully without complex technical jargon).
* 📕 [Making Things Happen: Mastering Project Management](https://ia803105.us.archive.org/1/items/makingthingshappenbyscottberkun/Making%20Things%20Happen%20by%20Scott%20Berkun%20.pdf) — (Insights on leadership, decision-making, and strategy in software and tech projects).

---

### 2. Time & Productivity Management ⏱️

* 📕 [Eat That Frog!: 21 Great Ways to Stop Procrastinating and Get More Done in Less Time](https://bdebooks.com/en/books/eat-that-frog-by-brian-tracy/#google_vignette) _by Brian Tracy_ — (A practical guide to overcoming procrastination and prioritizing tasks effectively).
* 📕 [Atomic Habits](https://dn790007.ca.archive.org/0/items/atomic-habits-pdfdrive/Atomic%20habits%20%28%20PDFDrive%20%29.pdf) _by James Clear_ — (An easy & proven way to build good habits & break bad ones).
* 📕 [Getting Things Done: The Art of Stress-Free Productivity](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/1633.Getting_Things_Done) _by David Allen_ — (A powerful framework for organizing tasks, reducing stress, and boosting efficiency).

* **[Notion](https://www.notion.so)** - All-in-one workspace for documentation, notes, and roadmaps.
