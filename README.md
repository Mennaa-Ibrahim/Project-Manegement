# Project-Manegement
Welcome! I'm Menna Ibrahim. This is a comprehensive project management roadmap I built and refined throughout my university journey.  
My goal is to make this guide rich, thorough, and resourceful for everyone. It includes a wide variety of curated resources and learning paths to help you grow. 



## 📚 Books & Reading List

Here is a curated collection of must-read books for anyone looking to master Product and Project Management—from foundational concepts to advanced execution strategies.

### 📂 Project Management Fundamentals

* **[A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK Guide)](https://www.academia.edu/63225348/A_guide_to_the_project_management_body_of_knowledge)** - by *Project Management Institute (PMI)* (The global standard and foundational reference for project management principles).
* **[Project Management for Non-Project Managers](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/13586821-project-management-for-non-project-managers)** - by *Jack Ferraro* (A practical guide to executing projects successfully without complex technical jargon).
* **[Making Things Happen: Mastering Project Management](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/223201.Making_Things_Happen)** - by *Scott Berkun* (Insights on leadership, decision-making, and strategy in software and tech projects).

### ⏱️ Time & Productivity Management

* **[Eat That Frog!: 21 Great Ways to Stop Procrastinating and Get More Done in Less Time](https://bdebooks.com/en/books/eat-that-frog-by-brian-tracy/#google_vignette)** - by *Brian Tracy* (A practical guide to overcoming procrastination and prioritizing tasks effectively).
* **[Atomic Habits](https://dn790007.ca.archive.org/0/items/atomic-habits-pdfdrive/Atomic%20habits%20%28%20PDFDrive%20%29.pdf)** - by *James Clear* (An easy & proven way to build good habits & break bad ones).
* **[Getting Things Done: The Art of Stress-Free Productivity](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/1633.Getting_Things_Done)** - by *David Allen* (A powerful framework for organizing tasks, reducing stress, and boosting efficiency).
