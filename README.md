## 📚 Books & Reading List

_A curated collection of must-read books for anyone looking to master Product and Project Management—from foundational concepts to advanced execution strategies._

#### 1. Project Management Fundamentals 📊

- 📕 **[A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK Guide)](https://www.academia.edu/63225348/A_guide_to_the_project_management_body_of_knowledge)** _by Project Management Institute (PMI)_  
  <small>— The global standard and foundational reference for project management principles.</small>
- 📕 **[Project Management for Non-Project Managers](https://tech.wayne.edu/project-management/pm-training-non-managers.pdf)**  
  <small>— A practical guide to executing projects successfully without complex technical jargon.</small>
- 📕 **[Making Things Happen: Mastering Project Management](https://ia803105.us.archive.org/1/items/makingthingshappenbyscottberkun/Making%20Things%20Happen%20by%20Scott%20Berkun%20.pdf)**  
  <small>— Insights on leadership, decision-making, and strategy in software and tech projects.</small>

---

#### 2. Time & Productivity Management ⏱️

- 📕 **[Eat That Frog!: 21 Great Ways to Stop Procrastinating](https://bdebooks.com/en/books/eat-that-frog-by-brian-tracy/#google_vignette)** _by Brian Tracy_  
  <small>— A practical guide to overcoming procrastination and prioritizing tasks effectively.</small>
- 📕 **[Atomic Habits](https://dn790007.ca.archive.org/0/items/atomic-habits-pdfdrive/Atomic%20habits%20%28%20PDFDrive%20%29.pdf)** _by James Clear_  
  <small>— An easy & proven way to build good habits & break bad ones.</small>
- 📕 **[Getting Things Done: The Art of Stress-Free Productivity](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/1633.Getting_Things_Done)** _by David Allen_  
  <small>— A powerful framework for organizing tasks, reducing stress, and boosting efficiency.</small>
